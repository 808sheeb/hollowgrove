Welcome to Hollowsgrove

This resting spot is maintained by the grovetender (he/him). A mysterious being, he has traversed many lands and yearned for the internet of old, memories of creative expression pushed him to create a beacon of outreach himself. He believes it to be a forever a work in progress. You may know his corporeal form, but in these realms, he likes to be considered simply as the grovetender.

... and look at this, a wonderful appearance from the grovetender himself.

I like to think of this as a blank canvas. In these lands that I've been curating you'll find all sorts of pathways and secret worlds created as tributes to media and IPs I enjoy. I also love to speak about things in both first & third person, so I hope you are of silly-mind and warm heart. These interlinked pages act as space for my whimsiest of world-building to occur.

This is a personal page dedicated to my web based creativity. I'm hoping to eventually share my computational poetry along with other projects of mine in the realms of creative technology, a/v works, and generative art. Oh and random pixel art & other mats that I want to share.

Thanks for visiting.
