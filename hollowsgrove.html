* HollowsGrove *

Welcome to HollowsGrove

This resting spot is maintained by the grovetender (he/him). A mysterious being, he has traversed many lands and yearned for the internet of old. Memories of creative expression pushed him to create a beacon himself. He believes it to be a forever a work in progress.

... and look at this, an appearance from the grovetender himself.

I like to think of this as a blank canvas. In these lands that I've been curating you'll find all sorts of pathways and secret worlds created as tributes to media and IPs I enjoy. I also love to speak about things in both first & third person, so I hope you are of silly-mind and warm heart. These interlinked pages act as space for my whimsiest of world-building to occur.

Dedicated to web based creativity, I'm hoping to eventually share my computational poetry along with other projects of mine in the realms of creative technology, a/v works, and generative art.

Thanks for visiting.
