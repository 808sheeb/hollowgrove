Shihab Mian (grovetender )

(she-hub me-in, he/him)

is a game developer, webweaver & creative technologist working primarily with creative programming & game engines. He is intrigued by the emotional dimensions of technology and how it shapes human connection in our increasingly networked world. In his practice, Shihab looks to deconstruct this relation between technology and humanity. Examining how it has been distorted, disfigured, and transformed by rapid technological advancement. He creates deprecated programs and intentional failings, where inefficiency, glitch, or error are embraced as part of the creative process.

further work can be found at shihabmian.com

On neocities & my other hand-made web weaving adventures, I like to practice my poetry and worldbuilding. I love to leave things mostly unfinished and consistently changing. I enjoy the diary like, identity embued nature of hand coded personal websites. Find easter eggs (obvious & obscure) of all my favorite shows, games, movies & music. Dithering and exploring javascript interactions within the web browser have been points of interest as of late.
